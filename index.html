01 · Background

About

I'm a computer science student at WPI who transferred from RPI, where I did controls development for Rensselaer Motorsport's Formula SAE team and served on the exec board of RPI Robotics. I build software for systems that have to be right the first time: feedback loops for robots, pipelines for teams, and the infrastructure in between.


My projects span a state-space simulator in C++, Merge Zone — a deployed repository analytics platform in Go — and a Unix-style media downloader. I'm most at home in the layers most people don't see: kernels, window managers, low-level networking. I like my tools measurable — what gets monitored gets improved.
